Gobbin

route a hungry goblin past the knights

A hungry goblin marches the dungeon tracks on his own, and you tap the junction levers to turn the rails under him. Route him onto gems, steer him clear of patrolling knights, and grab the crown gem to flip the whole hunt in your favour for a few wild seconds.

Get notified →

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap junction levers to turn the tracks.

  2. 2

    Route the goblin onto gems.

  3. 3

    Keep him clear of the knights.

You never touch the goblin directly. You just bend the world he runs through.
