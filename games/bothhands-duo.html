BothHands Duo

two phones, one Wi-Fi duel

The same duel, split across two phones on the same Wi-Fi. One of you takes the left paddle, the other takes the right, and the ball flies between your two screens in real time. No internet, no accounts, just the room you are both sitting in.

Get notified →

How to play

  1. 1

    Host a match; a friend joins over local Wi-Fi.

  2. 2

    Each player slides one paddle.

  3. 3

    First to the target score takes the room.

I wanted the kind of game you start by handing someone your phone.
